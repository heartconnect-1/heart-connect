# Tests

## Test 1 - Versioned legal acceptance and optional consent controls [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: GET /api/compliance/state, POST /api/compliance/accept, POST /api/compliance/consents, required Terms/Privacy/Community Guidelines gate, append-only consent history, Supabase compliance bridge
Description: Verifies members cannot enter the dating product without recording the current required policy versions and that optional marketing/privacy choices remain separate and reversible.
Setup:
1. Actor A is an authenticated adult Heart Connect member who has not accepted the current 2026-09-08-v1 policy set.
Steps:
1. Sign in as Actor A and verify member discovery is replaced by the current-policy gate with separate Terms, Privacy and Community Guidelines checkboxes and a Sign out option.
2. Accept all three required documents and continue; reopen Legal & Safety and verify the exact current versions appear as accepted.
3. Turn Marketing email on, leave Marketing SMS and Marketing push off, change Optional analytics on, save, refresh, then turn Marketing email off again.
4. Open consent history and verify both the grant and withdrawal are retained as separate history entries rather than overwriting the past event.
Expected: Required policy versions are recorded before normal member access, optional marketing is not preselected or bundled, current preferences persist, and consent changes retain a versioned history suitable for Supabase synchronization.

## Test 2 - Individual message/media reports and safety escalation
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: POST /api/report/:id for profile/message/media targets, confidential evidence reference, moderation queue creation, critical-priority child-safety escalation and evidence preservation
Description: Verifies message, photo and video reports preserve the exact target and that child-safety concerns enter urgent human review without automatic legal conclusions.
Setup:
1. Actor A and Actor B are adult mutual matches with at least one message from Actor B; Actor B has a visible profile photo and video.
Steps:
1. Actor A reports one specific message from Actor B for Scam/fraud and verifies the confirmation says the report was confidentially queued.
2. Actor A opens Actor B's profile and separately reports the visible photo and video; verify each action targets that media rather than silently converting to a whole-profile report.
3. Submit an Underage concern report and then, as an authorized safety reviewer, open the admin compliance queue.
4. Verify the underage report is Critical, has a child-safety case in urgent review, preserves evidence for a bounded period, and does not claim that Heart Connect automatically contacted an authority or proved the allegation.
Expected: Reports retain the correct target/evidence reference, enter the moderation queue, and child-safety concerns receive urgent human escalation without exposing the reporter or automating a legal conclusion.

## Test 3 - Suspension, restricted interactions, human appeal and overturn
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: POST /api/admin/moderation, GET /api/enforcements, POST /api/appeals, POST /api/compliance/admin/appeals/:id/review, interaction restriction, audit trail and member notification
Description: Verifies moderation restrictions are enforced in dating interactions while preserving member access to notices, appeals and human review.
Setup:
1. Actor Moderator is an authorized moderation staff member; Actor A is an ordinary adult member with a completed profile and a mutual match.
Steps:
1. Actor Moderator applies a temporary suspension to Actor A with a clear reason.
2. Actor A signs in and verifies discovery/connection interactions are restricted while Legal & Safety remains available and shows the enforcement reason plus an Appeal option.
3. Actor A submits an appeal of at least 10 characters and verifies duplicate open appeals for the same enforcement are rejected.
4. Actor Moderator or another authorized reviewer opens the appeals queue, overturns the action with a resolution, and Actor A refreshes.
Expected: Suspensions are enforceable rather than cosmetic, appeal access remains available, the reviewer decision is recorded, an overturned suspension is lifted, and no reporter identity is disclosed.

## Test 4 - Privacy, incognito and photo visibility persist and affect discovery
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: GET/POST /api/privacy, profileVisibility standard/incognito/paused, photoVisibility everyone/matches/private, existing visibility toggles and discovery enforcement
Description: Verifies privacy choices change actual discovery and media exposure rather than only changing local UI state.
Setup:
1. Actor A and Actor B are adult members; they are not initially matched.
Steps:
1. Actor A opens Privacy, sets Profile visibility to Incognito and Photo visibility to Mutual matches only, saves and refreshes.
2. As Actor B, verify Actor A is not discoverable until Actor A has liked Actor B; after that eligibility is met, verify Actor A can appear but media remains hidden until a mutual match exists.
3. After a mutual match, verify permitted media appears; Actor A then changes Photo visibility to Private and verify media is hidden again.
4. Actor A changes Profile visibility to Paused and verifies the profile leaves new discovery while existing account/legal controls remain accessible.
Expected: Privacy choices persist and materially control discovery/media exposure; incognito and paused modes are not decorative UI switches.

## Test 6 - Email reconfirmation uses short-lived hashed codes
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: POST /api/auth/security/email-code/request, POST /api/auth/security/email-code/verify, Resend delivery, code hashing, expiry, attempt limits, profile verification signal
Description: Verifies Heart Connect can reconfirm access to the signed-in provider email without storing the plain verification code or falsely accepting expired/incorrect codes.
Setup:
1. Actor A is signed in through the primary AppDeploy identity provider and has an email address; RESEND_API_KEY is configured.
Steps:
1. Open Account Security and request an email verification code; verify the UI displays only a masked email and a 10-minute validity message.
2. Enter an incorrect six-digit code repeatedly and verify attempts are rejected and bounded.
3. Request a new code and verify the previous challenge is replaced; enter the current correct code before expiry.
4. Refresh Account Security and the member profile and verify the email channel shows verified without exposing the code or private challenge data.
Expected: Resend sends from noreply@royal-heart.com, plain codes are never stored in application data, incorrect/expired codes fail, successful verification persists, and rate limits prevent rapid abuse.

## Test 7 - Phone verification fails closed until Twilio Verify is configured
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: POST /api/auth/security/phone-code/request, POST /api/auth/security/phone-code/verify, E.164 validation, Twilio Verify provider gating, private phone handling
Description: Verifies Heart Connect never pretends an SMS was sent when Twilio Verify is unavailable and stores only a hash plus last four digits after successful verification.
Setup:
1. Actor A is signed in. First run with one or more Twilio secrets absent; second run with TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID, TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN and TWILIO_VERIFY_SERVICE_SID configured.
Steps:
1. With Twilio disabled, enter +2547XXXXXXXX and request a code; verify the UI states phone verification is unavailable and no success state is shown.
2. With Twilio enabled, try a local-format or malformed number and verify it is rejected; then submit a valid E.164 number and receive a real Verify SMS.
3. Enter an incorrect/expired code and verify it is rejected and attempt limited; enter a current valid code and verify success.
4. Refresh Account Security and verify only the last four digits are displayed and the profile receives a phone-verification signal; the full number is not exposed as a public profile field.
Expected: Phone verification fails closed without provider credentials, uses Twilio Verify only when configured, validates international format, rate-limits abuse, and persists only a hash plus last four digits after approval.
