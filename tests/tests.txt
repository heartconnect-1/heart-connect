# Tests

## Test 1 - Adults-only registration, password policy and provider honesty [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: public Welcome/Create Account experience, POST /api/auth/register/preflight, 18+ enforcement, password-strength and password-match validation, Terms/Privacy acceptance, registration rate limiting, Supabase-ready provider status
Description: Verifies Heart Connect blocks underage registration, validates security requirements before any account request, and never pretends a Supabase account was created when the provider is not connected.
Steps:
1. Open Create Account, enter a date of birth that makes the member 17, complete the other required fields and submit.
2. Verify registration is blocked with an 18+ message; then use an adult date of birth but a weak or mismatched password and verify submission remains blocked.
3. Use an adult date of birth, matching strong password, phone, email, country, city, relationship intention and all required acceptances, then submit.
Expected: Under-18 and insecure submissions are rejected; an otherwise valid submission either continues to a real provider when configured or clearly states that Supabase is not connected, with no fake account or verification success.

## Test 2 - Sign-in, recovery, OTP and rate-limit failure paths
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: Sign In, Forgot Password, Account Recovery and Phone OTP UI; POST /api/auth/login/preflight, POST /api/auth/recovery/request, POST /api/auth/email/resend, POST /api/auth/phone/otp/request, POST /api/auth/phone/otp/verify; generic account-existence privacy; auth rate limiting; Google/Apple fallback
Description: Verifies credential and recovery surfaces do not leak whether an account exists and provider-dependent delivery remains truthful.
Steps:
1. Open Sign In and try email/password, then switch to phone sign-in and request an OTP.
2. Open Forgot Password and submit an arbitrary email or phone; verify the UI never confirms whether that identifier exists.
3. Open the OTP screen, enter an invalid 6-digit code and retry/resend within normal limits; verify provider-not-configured states do not claim an SMS was sent.
4. Use the Google or Apple secure sign-in fallback and verify the existing provider popup remains available.
Expected: Auth errors are visible, account existence remains private, rate limiting protects repeated requests, and no email or SMS is falsely reported as delivered.

## Test 3 - Protected member access, onboarding guard and logout confirmation
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: authenticated session restore, anonymous member-area denial by UI boundary, existing requireAuth backend protection, onboarding-before-discovery guard, current-device logout confirmation
Description: Verifies logged-out visitors remain in public auth/legal surfaces while authenticated members retain the existing profile-completion gate before discovery.
Setup:
1. Actor A is a valid adult Heart Connect member authenticated through the current secure provider.
Steps:
1. While signed out, verify Discover, Explore, Matches, Messages, Profile, Safe Date, Verification and Membership member surfaces are not exposed through the member navigation.
2. Sign in as Actor A with no completed dating profile and verify Heart Connect directs the member to complete profile/onboarding before discovery.
3. Open Account Security, choose Log out of this device, cancel the confirmation once, then confirm it.
Expected: Public/member boundaries hold, incomplete members do not enter discovery, cancelling logout keeps the session, and confirmed logout returns to the public authentication experience.

## Test 4 - Security center, role boundary and provider-dependent session controls
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: GET /api/auth/security, POST /api/auth/security/logout-all, change-password/update-email/update-phone/MFA provider gates, active-session display, suspicious-login alert surface, User/Support Agent/Moderator/Admin/Super Admin role representation, existing admin access restrictions
Description: Verifies private account security state is visible without overstating unsupported session revocation or MFA capability.
Setup:
1. Actor A is an ordinary authenticated member; Actor Admin is an authorized existing Heart Connect administrator.
Steps:
1. Actor A opens Account Security and verifies current device/browser, approximate location when available, last active and current-session status are shown privately.
2. Actor A attempts Logout all devices, Change password, Update email/phone and MFA setup while Supabase is not connected; verify each action explains the provider dependency and does not claim success.
3. Actor A attempts an existing admin-only operation and is denied; Actor Admin accesses the same authorized admin operation successfully.
Expected: Security controls are truthful, role boundaries remain least-privilege, and unsupported cross-device revocation or MFA cannot be presented as completed.

## Test 5 - Authentication, Legal & Safety Center, public contact routing and responsive accessibility
Viewport: desktop (1280x800)
Covers: existing profiles/discovery/messages/calls/safety/verification/location/Stage 1-8 regression after auth-shell upgrade, responsive auth forms, keyboard-focusable controls, public Legal & Safety Center with 42 policies, compact legal footer, public department contact directory, POST /api/contact Resend routing
Description: Verifies the authentication upgrade does not break the existing Heart Connect member application, while the public legal, contact and authentication experience remain usable on desktop and mobile.
Setup:
1. Actor A is an authenticated adult member with a completed profile and at least one mutual connection.
Steps:
1. Regression-check profile load, Discover/Explore, mutual connections, messaging, reporting, verification center, Location Center and existing Launch Center surfaces after sign-in.
2. Sign out and verify Welcome, Create Account, Sign In, Forgot Password, Terms and Privacy summaries render without member data.
3. Resize to mobile and keyboard-navigate registration/sign-in controls; verify fields, password visibility control, provider buttons and validation messages remain usable.
4. From the footer open the Legal & Safety Center, verify all 42 policies are searchable and category-filterable, open Romance Scam & Financial Fraud, AI & Automated Decision-Making and Child Safety / Minor Protection, then return to Welcome.
5. Scroll to Contact, verify the department directory includes info, support, admin, finance, marketing, careers, security, privacy, legal, safety and noreply addresses, then send a valid General enquiries message with subject "Heart Connect QA contact" and a complete message.
Expected: Existing member functionality remains available after authentication, private member data is absent from signed-out screens, auth surfaces remain responsive and keyboard-accessible, the Legal & Safety Center exposes the full 42-policy architecture without overwhelming the footer, and the contact form reports a successful routed message with a reference instead of falsely claiming delivery.
