## Test 1 - Personalization controls, recommendation explanations, reset and AI Matchmaker [sanity]
Viewport: mobile (375x667)
Covers: GET/POST /api/stage8/personalization, POST /api/stage8/personalization/reset, GET /api/stage8/recommendations, POST /api/stage8/recommendations/:id/feedback, POST /api/stage8/ai/matchmaker, GET /api/location/status, GET /api/location/me, GET /api/location/search, POST /api/location/manual, POST /api/location/profile, centralized Stage 8 intelligence eligibility, permanent LocationProvider fallback/migration, Stage 4 optional-learning reset integration, mobile Launch Center, Stage 1-7 discovery regression
Description: Verifies Stage 8 makes recommendation learning transparent and user-controlled while AI Matchmaker remains an eligibility-safe assistant rather than an autonomous matcher.
Setup:
1. Actor A is an adult Heart Connect member with a completed profile and eligible real profiles in discovery.
2. At least one candidate shares Actor A's explicit relationship intention or interests.
Steps:
1. Actor A opens Launch Center > Intelligence, turns Personalized Recommendations off, chooses a recommendation priority and refreshes; verify recommendations still use explicit preferences/compatibility and show Why you're seeing this.
2. Turn personalization on, submit More like this and Less like this feedback, and verify feedback is private and persists without becoming public profile data.
3. Choose Reset My Recommendations and confirm; verify optional Stage 8 feedback is cleared/ignored and Stage 4 optional activity learning is ignored before the reset timestamp, while safety/moderation records remain unchanged.
4. Open AI Matchmaker and ask for serious connections near Nairobi who want children; verify the request is translated only into approved filters and the shortlist contains at most five profiles that still satisfy adult, blocking, privacy, visibility and discovery eligibility.
5. Open Location Center and verify the existing city/country appears as a legacy fallback without data loss. Because MAPBOX_ACCESS_TOKEN is not required for safe fallback operation, verify provider status truthfully shows either Mapbox configured or Permanent manual fallback active. Save a manual city/county/country selection and refresh; verify the normalized location persists and legacy profile city/country update without exposing coordinates.\n6. If provider search is configured, type at least three characters and verify search is debounced/rate-limited and returns only provider-supported places; if unconfigured, verify the UI does not fabricate suggestions and manual entry remains available.\n7. Verify shortlist copy says AI-assisted recommendation and never says soulmate, perfect match or guaranteed compatibility; no connection is sent automatically.
Expected: Recommendation learning is visible, reversible and optional; reset does not delete safety records; AI Matchmaker cannot bypass discovery eligibility or auto-connect; and existing Discover/Explore remain functional.
